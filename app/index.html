      About

      and who it is built on

      Working out which clips are still missing can become guesswork in the last stretch of a completionist run.

      This works it out from your own save file, or from the clips you mark by hand when there is no save to read, on a console for instance. Either way it stays on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded, and no part of it is sent anywhere.

      It exists because other people solved the hard parts first and published what they found.

      Before you open this up

      one time only

      From here on the tracker stops being coy. It will name every clip, quote the first thing said in it, and show you a frame from it, including clips you have not found yet.

      It will spoil the game

      Finding out what is on these tapes is the whole of IMMORTALITY. This takes that away. There is no putting it back.

      Some of it is explicit

      The film contains nudity and violence, and the frames shown here are taken from it. Consider where you are sitting.

      You can go back to Total only at any time, and this will not be asked again.
